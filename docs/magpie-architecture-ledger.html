00 · THESIS⛓0x00 · a1c4

The whole system in one sentence

You don't need a new design — everything points at one architecture, now sharpened by a deep-research pass against the current state of the art. The spine: one signed append-only log is the sole source of truth; one small verified gate is the only thing that may write to it or act from it; everything else is a derived, regenerable projection.

L2+ · Projections derived · regenerable
Typed memory · retrieval pipeline · the wiki · dashboards · fine-tuning corpora · the robot's cognition. All rebuildable from the log — none of it precious.
L1 · The gate (deadbolt) the only writer
Sole authorizer of privileged events and actions. No ambient authority — capability in, witness out. Now also the memory-admission firewall.

Why this shape — and what the research changed

A physicist's instinct (conserve the true quantity, derive the rest) and a systems-builder's (event sourcing, capabilities, deterministic replay) land on the identical structure: the log is the conserved quantity; the gate is the conservation law; everything else is dynamics. The research pass didn't change the spine — it confirmed you're already at or ahead of SOTA on the architecture, and pointed the effort at two specific, measured wins (a reranker and a binary-question evaluator) instead of the things that feel impressive but aren't worth it (a full knowledge graph).

01 · LOG (L0)⛓0x01 · 7f3e

The hoard — the only irreplaceable asset skeleton

Ed25519-signed, hash-chained, append-only. Every observation, decision, action, consolidation, and sensor summary is an event. Doubles as audit and observability for free — and its durable home on the NAS is the backup of the only precious thing.

  • Append-only, never mutate. A correction is a new event that supersedes, not an edit. The history of what was believed and when it changed is kept.
  • Signed + chained. Tamper-evidence is structural — each event chains to the last, so the log can prove it hasn't been altered.
  • Boring and bulletproof first. The one layer you can't redo later. Get it dull, correct, and replayable before anything sits on top.
The governing invariant

Keep the irreplaceable set small. The log plus its provenance is the entire backup surface; every other store — the wiki, the vector index, the graph edges — can be deleted and rebuilt by replaying events. Longevity by reconstruction, not by hoarding the derived artifacts.

02 · GATE (L1)⛓0x02 · b71f

deadbolt — the kernel that decides ~200k LOC, in progress

The sole writer of privileged events and the only authorizer of privileged actions. An object-capability model: authority is an unforgeable reference, never ambient — linear logic in practice, which Rust's ownership already gives you. The governed loop is four moves:

01 · UNTRUSTEDSuggestAgent proposes an action as code.
02 · NORMALIZECompileHarness reduces it to a checkable form.
03 · AUTHORITYDecidedeadbolt checks capabilities + policy, then executes.
04 · PROOFWitnessA witness event is written to the log.
New job — the memory firewall (FragFuse defense)

Research surfaced a concrete threat for any agent memory: FragFuse fragments a forbidden request across benign-looking writes and reconstructs intent at retrieval time — ~86% bypass of naive access control, and prompt-injection and perplexity filters don't catch it. Because deadbolt sits outside the harness and every write is a signed event, it's the natural place to enforce the three-layer defense the paper calls for: admission control at write, capability checks at retrieval keyed to provenance, and a re-check of the assembled context packet at execution. This is a spot where Magpie can be ahead of the literature. (Defense is a recommended direction, not yet a benchmarked mitigation — treat as risk-reduction of unknown magnitude.)

Verify the small core, not the world

The gate is small on purpose so it can be formally verified with Verus (every privileged write has a witness; no escalation; the decision is pure) and made replayable with deterministic simulation testing. You verify the one component that holds authority — not the model, not the harness.

03 · MEMORY (L2)⛓0x03 · 5d92

Typed cognitive memory — the research differentiator designed

Everything here is a regenerable projection of the log. The research verdict: your hybrid substrate is the correct SOTA backbone — the highest-signal addition is a reranker, and the highest-signal non-action is skipping a full knowledge graph.

SUBSTRATE
One SQLite file — FTS5 (keyword) + sqlite-vec (vector) + entities/edges (graph) + provenance + temporal. Maintained by DBSP / differential dataflow: incremental views, not nightly re-index. Beats dedicated vector DBs well past any single corpus. Rust-native · formally verified
TYPED STORES
Episodic (the log, query-shaped) · Semantic claim graph · Procedural runbooks · Hypotheses & beliefs · Prospective. The last three are what most designs miss.
COMPILER
The context packet compiler assembles bounded, provenance-filtered context for the next action — and runs the FragFuse "does this fused context exceed the current grant?" check. Retrieval is its job, not the agent's.
PROJECTION
A Karpathy-style markdown wiki with provenance + claim-status frontmatter, plus Obsidian/git for human review. A projection fed by the substrate — never an intermediate truth the graph is built from.

The retrieval pipeline — where the measured win lives

The single best evidence-to-effort change in the whole report. Keep the hybrid backbone; add a cross-encoder reranker as the final stage over the fused candidates:

FUSERRFReciprocal Rank Fusion — robust, near-zero tuning.
RERANK ★Cross-encoderRe-score top ~50–100. The biggest lever.

Why the reranker is Tier-1

Adding a cross-encoder rerank to hybrid retrieval measured +17.2pp MRR@3 and +12.1pp Recall@5 in a 2026 benchmark — repeatedly the single most impactful RAG component, typically +5–15 nDCG@10. Rust-buildable today via fastembed-rs (TextRerank, ONNX). Honest threshold: if on your eval it adds <3pp Recall@5, drop it — that means your fused pool already contains the answer. measured

The epistemic spectrum + bi-temporal supersession

The axis that makes this a research memory, not a notes app. Every item carries a status; evidence moves it, each transition logged as an event. Contradiction handling is the same move the SOTA temporal-KGs use — invalidate with a validity interval, never delete:

openconjecturedsupportedsettled / provenrefuted
Hypotheses & beliefs
the live, conjectural end — working theories held distinctly from settled fact.
Semantic claim graph
the settled end — entities and relations treated as known.
You already generalize the one KG idea worth keeping

Bi-temporal validity (Zep/Graphiti) — tracking when a fact was true and superseding rather than deleting — drove up to 18.5% accuracy gains on long-memory benchmarks. Your epistemic spectrum + append-only supersession is a strict generalization of it. Wire supported → refuted to emit an invalidation event with a validity interval. measured (vendor)

The high-value non-action: skip the full knowledge graph

GraphRAG's gains are real but confined to global-summary / aggregation queries — a controlled benchmark showed the "graph win" collapsing from 73.5% to 23.1% (near vector-RAG) once query-time aggregation was removed, i.e. the win was the aggregation, not the graph. For the entity-lookup and multi-session recall a research mind actually does, a heavy typed-edge graph isn't worth the build-and-maintain cost. If you ever need "what are the themes across all my memory?", add LazyGraphRAG-style community detection at query time over your existing edges — don't pre-build a graph to maintain.

The dream cycle is validated "sleep-time compute"

Your decay → contradiction/supersession → merge → synthesis pass (writing new events, never mutating) is exactly the offline-consolidation pattern that measured +13–18% reasoning accuracy and ~2.5× lower per-query cost by precomputing context during idle time. The agent gets sharper while it sleeps. Static-embedding models (model2vec-rs, ~92% of MiniLM quality at ~500× CPU speed) make the high-volume passes cheap. measured

05 · LOOPS⛓0x05 · e2d6

Loop engineering — brakes before horsepower design

The leverage moved from the prompt to the loop, and from typing to judgment. A working loop is six pieces; a safe one is six pieces plus brakes you install before the engine.

  • State — the only thing the next run inherits. One file: done / in-progress / next / never-touch.
  • Automations — what makes it a loop, not a thing you ran once.
  • Worktrees — isolate parallel agents so they don't write the same file.
  • Skills — your intent written once, outside, so the agent stops guessing.
  • Connectors — let the loop act, not just talk.
  • Sub-agents — split the maker from the checker, different model. The highest-value move in any loop.
Local inference flips the economics

The cautionary tales — a $47k eleven-day runaway, a $1.3M/month fleet — are priced for frontier cloud agents. On the hub your marginal cost is electricity (~$2/month at 6h/day). The five-figure disaster can't happen; the worst case is wasted watt-hours and a wedged worktree.

But three deaths aren't about money brakes still apply

Runaway recursion

Two agents feed each other forever. Cure: step cap + budget ceiling.

Silent death

Hits the context wall, keeps "resuming" while looking alive. Cure: heartbeat + fresh context per phase.

Random walk

No verifiable stop condition, so it drifts. Cure: a fixpoint it can reach — a passing test, not "looks done."

Comprehension debt

Ships faster than you understand; you become a rubber stamp. Cure: a human-read gate it can't skip.

Brakes to bolt on before walking awaynon-negotiable
  • Step cap — --max-turns 50, hard stop.
  • Budget ceiling — per phase (caps wasted time even on local).
  • Blast radius — one worktree, one branch, read-only outside src/.
  • Circuit breaker — same tool + same args ×3 = halt.
  • Dead-man heartbeat — write a pulse each run; silence pages you.
06 · OVERSIGHT⛓0x06 · 9a40

Judge, then improve — never the reverse design

The research pass put hard numbers on the instinct you already had: build the evaluator before the self-improver. A weak judge doesn't just slow you down — it actively breeds agents that learn to fool it. Two channels keep SOTA in an otherwise-local loop; under them sits a calibrated evaluator.

The evaluator — binary questions, not holistic scores

BINEVAL → the epistemic spectrum

Decomposing evaluation into atomic yes/no questions (BINEVAL) matches or beats holistic judges (G-Eval, UniEval), is better calibrated, and avoids the ceiling effects that make 1–10 judge scores mush. The payoff for Magpie: binary verdicts are the evidence atoms your epistemic spectrum needs — a claim's status becomes a deterministic, logged function of accumulated yes/no answers, each with provenance. That's a debuggable audit trail of why a claim moved, which a holistic score can never give. Build it native in Rust — it's prompt orchestration over your log. measured

① PR review panelevent · pull · per-diff

A frontier model fires only when there's a concrete artifact to judge. Claude (claude-code-action@v1), Codex, CodeRabbit — across different model families for catch-rate diversity. Gate via an on: pull_request_review aggregator, feed the maker, re-review — hard cap on rounds, human tie-break. This is RQGM's diverse agent-as-judge panel.

② Heartbeattime/phase · push · over trajectory

A periodic frontier check on the loop's direction, not a single artifact — the brake against the deaths a per-diff reviewer is blind to. Feed it state, not logs. Give it teeth (halt / re-plan / escalate). Cadence by cost-of-being-wrong.

Gate Champion evolution behind an adversarial judge — the numbers say why

The RQGM paper measured the exact failure you intuited: the strongest baseline reviewer over-accepts AI-generated work at 1.91× the human rate, and champion-challenger search against a weak judge evolves judge-foolers. The fix is RQGM's epoch discipline — freeze the judge within an epoch, evolve it (adversarially, cross-family) only at boundaries, log every utility change to L0. Your council's adversary role exists to find judge-foolers before a champion is promoted. Hard gate: no Champion evolution until the panel shows zero over-acceptance gap between known-good and adversarial submissions. Co-evolved graders measured +9% ground-truth accuracy. measured

Prompt optimization — the one Python seam

DSPy/GEPA turns the evaluator's question-level feedback into improved prompts — measured +6pp avg (up to +19pp) over GRPO and >10pp over MIPROv2, at ~35× fewer rollouts (task-dependent). It's Python-only, so run it offline during the dream cycle and have it emit optimized prompts as new signed L0 artifacts that deadbolt admits. Don't put it in the hot path. measured
